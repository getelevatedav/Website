    Good to know

    Multiview

    The base system includes one multiview tiler. It builds one layout of up to four sources that can go to any number of TVs, and every TV showing multiview shares that layout. Layouts are programmed in advance and chosen from the tablet.

    Existing cabling

    One new Cat6 run is included. TV allowances assume short connections from each receiver to its TV. If we find in-wall HDMI runs or hard access, that work is priced and presented for your approval before it starts.

    Scheduling

    Work is scheduled around your 11:00 AM open. The switchover from the old system to the new one is done before opening or during your slowest window, confirmed with you in advance.

    What you keep

    A labeled rack, an equipment list, and a written record of how every source, TV and audio input is connected, plus a walkthrough of the tablets for your staff.

    Pricing

    The figures here are a preliminary estimate. After the site walk and distributor quote you'll get one fixed, turnkey price. A 50% deposit reserves your dates; the balance is due on completion.

    No handheld remote

    Control runs through the two tablet stations, so there's one consistent way to run the room on every shift.

    System details

    For the technically curious
    Video system
    • Three Just Add Power MC-TX1 4K60 transmitters at the cable boxes; one more per streaming source added.
    • Ten Just Add Power MC-RX1 4K60 receivers, one per TV, each with a new HDMI patch lead.
    • One Just Add Power MC-QX 4K60 tiler (up to four sources per tiled screen).
    • Managed Ethernet switch with multicast/IGMP support, sized after reviewing your existing network and ports.
    • Right-angle HDMI adapters where mounts or clearance require them; direction confirmed at each TV.
    • Control4 Core 3 for source routing, multiview presets and IR control of the cable boxes.
    Audio system
    • Triad TS-AMS8-V2: eight stereo inputs, eight outputs; analog RCA, digital coax and optical inputs; line-level feed to the main system.
    • Cable-box audio connections confirmed against exact box models; any extraction specified after that check.
    • Sonos Port by digital coax where compatible, RCA as fallback.
    • Dedicated jukebox audio path to the rack; line-level interface specified if it must travel over Cat6.
    DJ / microphone input path (add-on)

    Two wall-mounted XLR inputs feed an Audinate Dante AVIO 2-channel analog input adapter. Audio travels over the network to a matching Dante output adapter at the rack, which feeds an analog input on the matrix. The matrix doesn't accept Dante directly.

    • Adapters take line-level audio. Mics that need a preamp require a small mixer or preamp, priced separately.
    • Alternate Dante products are confirmed for model and availability before substituting.
